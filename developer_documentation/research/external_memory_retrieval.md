# External addressable memory: byte-native retrieval for a small core (IDEA 2)

## Abstract

IDEA 2 is the exact-recall engine behind the trillion-character context goal: a small byte core that addresses any span of a very large history by retrieving it, rather than holding it in attention or in a fixed recurrent state. The mechanism is a trained byte-native key head over the frozen trunk's pooled residual, the model shapes its own address space, with no tokenizer and no external embedder, plus a prefix-inject read that places the retrieved leaf into the generation context. This document is the honest synthesis of what the 2026-07 experiment campaign validated and what it killed. The short version: retrieval works and generalizes to unseen real topics, the index scales sub-quadratically to trillion-character feasibility on one box, and the end-to-end bottleneck is now precisely located, top-1 retrieval precision on natural queries, and the small generator's inability to disambiguate among candidates. Two tempting shortcuts were falsified along the way. The findings live in `successes.md` / `failures.md` by date; this doc ties them into one story and states the productionization plan.

## What the mechanism is

A leaf is a short byte chunk (~2 KB). Its key is produced by running the chunk through the hybrid trunk, mean-pooling the residual before the byte-projection head, and passing that through a small trained key head (MLP 1024 -> 512 -> 256, unit-normed). A query is keyed the same way. Retrieval is cosine top-k over the key store. The retrieved leaf's raw bytes are injected as a `context:` prefix and the core generates conditioned on them. The key head is trained contrastively (InfoNCE) with store-distractor negatives over a frozen trunk, so it adds a thin, cheap addressing layer without disturbing the language model. Everything is byte-native: keys are learned jointly against real store distractors, not borrowed from a subword embedder.

The design ambition (dual memory with a learned gist-vs-exact gate, a learned hierarchical drill-down navigator, retrieved bytes folded into the recurrent state) is recorded in `ideas.md` under IDEA 2. What follows is what was actually built and measured, which is the retrieval-and-inject core of that design, plus the scale path.

## What is validated

**Retrieval works end-to-end (needle recall).** A trained key head makes long-context needle recall work where a zero-training mean-pool baseline does not: the head is the operative piece, not the pooling. This cleared IDEA 2 milestone-1 (`successes.md` 2026-07-11).

**It generalizes to real content across unseen topics.** Trained on real claims from 33 topics and tested on 5 completely held-out real topics over a 1e5-leaf fineweb store, the head reaches recall@1 0.70 / recall@5 0.92 versus a raw baseline of 0.07 / 0.16, a ~10x lift, and the unseen-topic number matches the seen-topic number, so it is genuine topic transfer, not memorization. Under a harder distractor pool (fineweb plus thousands of OTHER real facts, which are semantically competitive) held-out recall is 0.50 / 0.78, lower as expected but still 7x over baseline (`successes.md` 2026-07-13). The governing law, established across the campaign, is diversity -> transfer: 11 invented schemas gave weak transfer (0.33), 33 diverse real topics gave strong transfer (0.70).

**Natural queries transfer, but are harder, and the end-to-end path is retrieval-bound.** The last open boundary was that all the above used heuristic "What is X?" queries. Measured against natural teacher-written questions (qwen-generated, held-out topics), the head still transfers at ~3x baseline, but natural queries are ~2x harder: hard-store recall@1 falls to 0.32 (from 0.50 heuristic). End-to-end, grounded answering beats bare 14x (0.14 vs 0.01) but is only 14% absolute, and that ceiling is set by retrieval, not the generator: recall@1 ~0.30 caps how often the right fact can reach the core (`successes.md` 2026-07-13). This is the honest recalibration, the mechanism retrieves and generalizes, but on natural free-form queries at small scale the absolute numbers are modest and retrieval-limited.

**The index scales sub-quadratically, and a trillion-character index is feasible on-box.** A pure-numpy hierarchical IVF drill-down keeps recall within ~1.5 points of exact while scoring ~30x fewer candidates, with candidate cost growing as sqrt(N), sub-quadratic (`successes.md` 2026-07-12). FAISS IVF-PQ realizes the wall-time win the numpy loop could not: per-query 155x faster than a flat scan at N=1e7, with the speedup GROWING with N, and 32x smaller memory via PQ. A trillion characters is ~500M leaves; 500M keys at 32-64 B PQ is 16-32 GB RAM at sub-millisecond query, it fits on one M3 Ultra (`successes.md` 2026-07-13). The trillion-scale target is an engineering build (an hours-long embedding pass), not an open research question.

## What is falsified

**RARS, folding retrieved bytes into the O(1) recurrent state, fails for exact recall.** The recurrent state saturates; it cannot hold verbatim spans the way the design hoped. Exact recall must come from the addressable read, not from compressing the read back into constant state (`failures.md` 2026-07-13).

**The key head overfits low-diversity invented schemas.** With too few, too-similar training schemas the head learns the schema shape rather than retrieval-in-general and does not transfer. This is a low-diversity artifact, resolved by the diversity -> transfer law, not a failure of the mechanism (`failures.md` 2026-07-13).

**Multi-leaf (top-k) context injection HURTS the small generator.** The obvious lever off the natural-query numbers, recall@5 (~0.63) is ~2x recall@1 (~0.37), so inject the top-k leaves and convert the extra coverage into grounding, backfires. Measured on chat200m, grounded_acc is 0.130 at top-1, 0.120 at top-3, 0.080 at top-5: more candidate leaves monotonically reduce grounding. The cause is that a 200M copy-limited generator cannot select which of k leaves holds the answer; the k-1 distractors mislead it. This localizes the end-to-end bottleneck precisely, it is top-1 PRECISION plus generator disambiguation, not recall@k coverage, and it prevented a net-negative serving change (raising the injected-leaf count) (`failures.md` 2026-07-13).

## The honest position

Retrieval is real and generalizes; the scale path is real and on-box feasible. The end-to-end answer quality is gated by two things that are now named plainly. First, top-1 retrieval precision on natural queries is modest (~0.32 hard-store), the right leaf is usually in the top-5 (~0.61) but not reliably first. Second, the current generator (chat200m) is copy-limited: it needs a single clean leaf and cannot disambiguate among candidates, which is why the recall@5 headroom does not convert. Both are addressed by the 800M pretrain in flight, a stronger core issues better queries (higher precision) and can plausibly select among candidates directly. The re-ranker sub-idea (collapse top-k to the single best leaf before injection) is logged in `ideas.md` as the fallback if the 800M still needs single-leaf precision; it is not built, because it competes with the 800M for the same bottleneck and the 800M is the higher-leverage fix already running.

Crucially, both bottlenecks were probed with a cheap RECIPE fix and both recipes failed, which is what points the finger at scale rather than tuning. On the generator side, injecting more candidate leaves (top-k) made grounding worse, not better (`failures.md` 2026-07-13). On the retrieval side, hard-negative mining, the textbook lever for exactly this "gold leaf beaten by a near-competitor" failure, did not lift hard-store recall@1 (baseline 0.351 -> mined 0.327, within noise; `failures.md` 2026-07-13), because the limit is the frozen trunk's feature resolution on semantically-competitive facts, not the negative-sampling strategy. Two independent recipe levers, both dead, both pointing at the same fix: a richer trunk. That is the 800M, and it is why the honest position is "the mechanism is validated, the remaining gain is scale," not "the mechanism needs more tuning."

## Productionization plan (staged, 800M-gated for real quality)

The integration surface is a single function. Serving today retrieves with a BM25 lexical index (a deliberate no-embedder, no-ollama design) through `retrieve(message, k, scope)` in `veritate_mri/routes/hybrid_routes.py`, which returns `(chunk_previews, scores)` and feeds `build_prompt`'s `context:` block. The neural memory system plugs in entirely behind that one function, with the same signature and return type, nothing in `build_prompt`, `_render_local`, or the streaming path changes. The work to ship it:

1. **Persist the key head.** The evals train a head on the fly; serving needs a saved head checkpoint loaded once at startup, reusing the model the serving process already holds.
2. **Build a KB key store.** Embed each KB chunk (trunk -> head -> unit-norm key) and index it (FAISS IVF-PQ at scale; a flat store is fine for small KBs), cached per scope alongside the existing `_STATE` BM25 cache, invalidated on upload like `reset_index_cache`.
3. **Swap `retrieve` internals** to embed the query and score against the key store, returning the same preview/score shape. BM25 can remain as a fallback or a hybrid re-rank signal.
4. **Inject top-1, not top-k, for the small local model.** The falsification is explicit: for a weak generator single-leaf injection wins. `TOP_K` / `CTX_LEAVES` stays a per-model knob, a larger platform-served model may benefit from more leaves; re-run `eval_teacher_topk.py` per generator before raising it.

This swap is a design change to production serving (it introduces a neural embedder into a path that was deliberately lexical), so it is staged, not shipped unattended. Real end-to-end quality waits on the 800M generator regardless; the retrieval upgrade is model-agnostic and can land first when a session is available to verify it live.
