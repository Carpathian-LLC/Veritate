Cross-sectional momentum — weekly long/short

checking...
How it was validated: net-positive in every out-of-sample year at real fees (Sharpe 0.44 baseline). The vol-gated arm scored Sharpe 0.93 in testing, but that gate was picked on the test set - thin evidence - so both arms run forward and the live record decides.
The one strategy that stayed positive in every out-of-sample year of the research campaign, now proving itself forward with simulated capital. Once a week it ranks 40 liquid coins by trailing 7-day return, goes long the 8 strongest and short the 8 weakest in equal size, and pays 3.5 bps a side. Two arms trade the same rule: Baseline is always in the market; Vol-gated steps to cash whenever Bitcoin's 30-day volatility runs above its one-year median. The gate scored best in testing but on thin evidence, so both run and the live record decides. Rebalances Mondays (UTC); judge it in weeks, not days.

ML weekly book (7-day ranks)

checking...
How it was validated: positive in every walk-forward year 2022-2026 at real fees, including with one-day delayed entry (~13%/yr, Sharpe ~1.0, max drawdown ~12%), and it survived an adversarial audit: leakage hunt, longer train/test gap, portfolio-size changes, seed variance, funding costs, entry lag. Caveats: simulated capital; a backtest is an upper bound; seed-to-seed variance means bumpy months are normal; the forward record starts 2026-07-04 and is the test that counts.
A machine-learned ranking of 40 liquid coins, rebuilt daily. A frozen model (three seeds averaged, trained on history through the latest complete month, retrained quarterly) reads 28 features of each coin's recent behavior and scores its odds of rising over the next week; the book goes long the ~8 it rates strongest and short the ~8 weakest (the top and bottom fifth of whatever names have live data), holding each day's picks for a week. The book is 7 daily slices: only one slice trades per day (after the UTC close), so turnover stays low at 3.5 bps a side. Entries lag the signal by construction, which is exactly the delayed-entry variant the audit validated. Judge it in weeks, not days.

US equity momentum — monthly 12-1

checking...
How it was validated: backtest beat SPY 7 of the last 10 years and ran +5.6pp/yr over survivorship-matched random books. Treat that as an upper bound: free historical data cannot fully rule out survivorship bias, so the forward record here is the proof that counts.
The most-documented factor in finance, validated forward from a universe frozen on 2026-07-03 (~500 large caps) because free historical data cannot rule out survivorship bias. On the first trading session of each month it ranks every name by its return from 12 months ago to 1 month ago (skipping the latest month, the standard spec) and holds the top ~50 equal-weight, long only, 3 bps a side. Backtest: beat SPY 7 of the last 10 years and beat 20 survivorship-matched random books — treat that as an upper bound; the forward record here is the real test. Trades only in US market hours; judge it in months.
